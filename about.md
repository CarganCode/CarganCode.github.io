## The short version

I have just submitted my PhD in Computer Science at the University of Nottingham as part of the Computational Optimisation and Learning Lab (2019 - 2026, viva January 2027), funded by an EPSRC studentship and supervised by Dr Isaac Triguero and Prof Dario Landa-Silva. My thesis, *Learning to Predict for Optimisation: from Predict-then-Optimise to Predict-and-Optimise*, focuses on optimal decision-making and planning under uncertainty. Alongside my PhD, I work part-time in industry at Acora (formerly Elastacloud) on our agentic enterprise search product.

I'm international; I have lived all over the place. I'm one of those people that, when you ask where they are from, lets out a sigh and asks if you're sure you want to know. I was born in Brighton, England, and have spent time living in Singapore, China, and New York, USA (if you couldn't tell from the photos).
I love travelling and have been fortunate enough to visit many places all over the world and meet some truly remarkable people along the way.

I also enjoy photography, building projects on the side ([Projects](/projects/)), and spending weekends climbing. Feel free to browse some of my photos in the [Gallery](/gallery/).

## The long version

I have always had an interest in computers and how they work. I started programming when I was 14 (in Visual Basic). My foray into coding happened to line up with my move to Singapore, where I met teachers who really supported me and helped foster a real passion and interest in the subject. It was thanks to the skills I developed and the people I met in Singapore that, when I moved to New York, I was introduced to Andrew Stillman and ended up interning at New Visions for Public Schools. There I worked with Google Apps Script and took ownership of autoCrat, a Google Sheets document-merge tool, rebuilding the engine from scratch as part of the early-access pre-release group for the Google Sheets Add-ons store (making plenty of mistakes and learning a lot along the way). <!-- TODO: add a sentence on TeenTech NY if you want to include it -->

When I came back to the UK, I studied Computer Science and AI with a Year in Industry at the University of Nottingham (2015 - 2019, graduating with First-Class Honours, the Best Dissertation 2019 award, and an Undergraduate High Achiever Award). At the same time I met Richard Conway, one of the founders of Elastacloud (now part of Acora) <!-- TODO: add a short note on how you met Richard Conway -->, where I interned and completed my placement year.
After graduating, I stayed on part-time, progressing from Data and ML Engineer through to Tech Lead / Senior Data Scientist.

My [undergraduate dissertation](/projects/ids/) on forecasting solar irradiance led straight into my PhD at Nottingham, which I started just as COVID began and so decided to pair it with part-time work at Elastacloud/Acora (to help keep some sanity).
I have been lucky (or unlucky) enough to set the direction of my own research, and over the course of the PhD it evolved from time-series and satellite forecasting into decision-focused learning, combinatorial optimisation, and reinforcement learning in JAX and Flax.

## Technical Focus

* **Programming & ML Stack**: Python (JAX, Flax, Optax, Orbax, PyTorch), SQL, Bash, Apache Spark.
* **Research**: Reinforcement learning (policy gradient, PPO, GRPO-style advantages), neural combinatorial optimisation, decision-focused learning, multimodal and time-series forecasting.
* **Systems & Infrastructure**: LLM evaluation benchmarks, agentic tool-calling systems, retrieval-augmented generation, SLURM, GPU profiling (XLA), Azure, GCP, Git, CI/CD.
