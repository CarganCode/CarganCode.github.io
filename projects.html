Research

PhD Research: Learning to Predict for Optimisation

My PhD traces the progression from decoupled Predict-then-Optimise pipelines to integrated Predict-and-Optimise systems. Covers global multi-site solar irradiance forecasting with Encoder-Decoder Transformers, evolutionary directed perturbations for surrogate decision-focused learning (IJCNN 2025), and RL-trained neural combinatorial optimisation policies in JAX and Flax used as task-gradient operators.

Directed Perturbations for Surrogate DFL | 2025

Code accompanying our IJCNN 2025 paper. Uses an evolutionary perturbation strategy to focus surrogate loss sampling near decision boundaries, reaching surrogate convergence with up to 4x fewer solver evaluations on a solar-plus-battery dispatch benchmark.

Local-Global Solar Forecasting | 2024

Experimental framework for our Applied Intelligence (2024) study on global cross-learning across distributed solar plants, including holdout site generalisation (GPH) and sensor-outage substitution (GPkN).

Open-Source Libraries & Tools

chemise | 2022 - 2025

My training framework for Flax models in JAX, built to eliminate repetitive training-loop boilerplate while supporting multi-GPU data and model parallelism, checkpointing, metric logging, and dataset integration. I used it across my research experiments. (GitHub repository)

GPU-Accelerated Genetic Algorithms in JAX | 2023

Vectorised genetic algorithm built in JAX using jax.vmap, jax.jit, and jax.lax.scan to eliminate host-loop compilation overhead and batch 10,000 problem instances across a single GPU. (GitHub profile)

EUMETSAT Downloader | 2020 - 2024

Tooling to download and preprocess high-resolution, high-frequency SEVIRI imagery from EUMETSAT MSG satellites for scalable ML training pipelines on SLURM clusters.
