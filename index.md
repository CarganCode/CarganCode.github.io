## Overview

I am a Computer Science PhD researcher at the University of Nottingham (thesis submitted October 2026; viva January 2027) and a Tech Lead and Senior Data Scientist at Acora (formerly Elastacloud), with over seven years of part-time industry experience alongside doctoral research.

My work focuses on reinforcement learning systems in JAX and Flax, decision-focused learning, neural combinatorial optimisation under uncertainty, multimodal forecasting, and evaluation infrastructure for machine learning and LLM systems.

## Selected Work

* **[PhD Research: Predict-and-Optimise](/projects/phd/)**: Thesis *Learning to Predict for Optimisation: from Predict-then-Optimise to Predict-and-Optimise*. Uses RL-trained neural combinatorial solvers in JAX/Flax as task-gradient operators, alongside evolutionary surrogate decision-focused learning (IJCNN 2025) and multimodal solar irradiance forecasting (*Applied Intelligence*, 2024).
* **[chemise](/projects/chemise/)** ([GitHub](https://github.com/TimCargan/chemise)): Open-source training framework for Flax models in JAX, supporting multi-GPU data and model parallelism, checkpointing, and metric logging.
* **[Industry Engineering at Acora](/projects/industry/)**: Tech Lead for an enterprise search team, maintaining a shared Python SDK across production and offline evaluation, a 1,000+ task LLM evaluation benchmark, and agentic tool-calling pipelines.
* **[Technical Writing](/blog/)**: Notes on decision-focused learning, GPU-accelerated genetic algorithms in JAX, and Python engineering.

Outside research and engineering, I am an occasional rock climber and amateur photographer ([Gallery](/gallery/), [About](/about/)).
