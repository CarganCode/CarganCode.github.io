## The Basics

Hi, I'm Tim.
I have just submitted my [PhD](/projects/phd/) in the School of Computer Science at the University of Nottingham (viva in January 2027), researching decision-focused learning and neural combinatorial optimisation in JAX and Flax. Alongside my PhD, I have over seven years of part-time industry experience and am currently a Tech Lead and Senior Data Scientist at [Acora](/projects/industry/) (formerly Elastacloud), building agentic enterprise search and evaluation systems.

I have always been interested in computers and technology. Personally I blame my dad for letting me play on his computer when I was young. My passion has led me to have some fantastic opportunities and meet some truly remarkable people. I love writing clean code, solving interesting problems and taking pictures, and I find myself most productive with a fresh cup of coffee and some _sick tunes_.

In my spare time, I'm an occasional climber and amateur photographer; you can check out some of my favourite shots in my [Gallery](/gallery/).

## What I'm up to

Most likely lost in a tensor (or profiling an XLA trace).

Ahead of my viva, I'm wrapping up my [PhD work](/projects/phd/) on RL-trained neural combinatorial solvers in JAX and Flax as task-gradient operators, alongside earlier work on evolutionary surrogate decision-focused learning (IJCNN 2025) and multimodal solar irradiance forecasting (*Applied Intelligence*, 2024). I also maintain [chemise](/projects/chemise/), my open-source training framework for Flax models ([GitHub](https://github.com/TimCargan/chemise)), and lead data science engineering at [Acora](/projects/industry/) across a shared Python SDK, a 1,000+ task LLM evaluation benchmark, and agentic tool-calling pipelines.

Check out my [Projects](/projects/), browse the [Blog](/blog/), or read a bit more [About](/about/) me. You can also find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/timcargan), [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=O9O4vwUAAAAJ), and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/timcargan).
